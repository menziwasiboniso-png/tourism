About Lesotho

Discover the history, people and culture of the Kingdom in the Sky.

The Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a landlocked country in southern Africa, completely surrounded by South Africa. Its high altitude, mountain ranges and traditional villages make it a distinctive destination.

The country is known for its Basotho people, the traditional Basotho blanket, the mokorotlo hat and its strong cultural heritage.

Visitors can explore mountains, historical sites, dams, caves and villages while learning about local traditions.

Interesting Facts

Capital City

Maseru is the capital and largest city of Lesotho.

Official Languages

Sesotho and English are the official languages. Other languages such as isiXhosa and Siphuthi are also spoken in Lesotho.

Mountain Kingdom

Lesotho is famous for its high elevation and dramatic mountain landscapes.

Quick Facts

Official name Kingdom of Lesotho
Capital Maseru
Currency Loti (plural: Maloti), pegged to the South African rand
Languages Sesotho and English (official); isiXhosa and Siphuthi are also spoken
Highest point Thabana Ntlenyana, 3,482 m
Founder of the nation King Moshoeshoe I, at Thaba Bosiu

Basotho Culture

Basotho culture is expressed through music, dance, crafts, clothing, storytelling and community traditions. Visitors should respect local customs and ask permission before photographing people.
